04Model comparison and selection

Four criteria at the headline cell — conditional-coverage p-value, coverage error, mean VaR as a share of NAV, and pinball loss — ranked 1 (best) to 4 and averaged. Lowest average rank is the model to deploy.

05Stress testing

Realised crisis episodes and documented out-of-sample drawdowns, each expressed as a multiple of one normal bad day. Stress is absolute, so it does not move with the filters above.

06Reverse stress test

Fix the loss, solve for the market move that causes it — the board's question: how far does the index fall before we lose a fifth of the fund?

Method. Equal-weighted book of 10 large-cap US equities, monthly rebalance, phased in from ₹1,000,000. Historical-simulation VaR/ES at 95/99/99.5% over 250/500/750-day windows, scaled by EWMA, GARCH(1,1) and GJR-GARCH conditional volatility (estimated with arch, robust Bollerslev–Wooldridge standard errors). Backtests: Kupiec POF, Christoffersen independence and conditional coverage, Basel traffic light. Stress: CAPM-beta overlay, volatility and correlation shocks, sector shock, reverse stress.

Sections 01–04 respond to the filters above; 05–06 are absolute. Every chart has a table view. Not investment advice.
